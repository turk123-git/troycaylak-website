The Person

About Troy

Troy Caylak is an actor based in Los Angeles who has somehow ended up in scenes with Bill Hader, Chris Evans, Kiefer Sutherland, Chris Meloni, Mandy Moore, Christina Applegate, Craig Robinson, and Lil Dicky. None of them asked him to leave. That's the resume.

He does drama. He does comedy. He does that specific thing where you can't tell which one it is but it's working and nobody wants to stop it. Casting directors have a word for this. Troy just calls it Tuesday.

HBO twice. Netflix. FX. NBC. CBS. ABC. Peacock twice. Paramount+. Amazon. If there's a network or streamer that hasn't called yet, it's only a matter of time. He's patient. He's got credits to spare.
